About Me

I am a data professional with experience in data engineering, data migration, data analytics, quality control, and business intelligence. My background combines strong analytical skills from a Mathematics background with hands-on experience in Python, SQL, ETL, data processing, data validation, and reporting.

In my previous role as a Data Migration Consultant, I worked on end-to-end data migration projects involving legacy systems and cloud-based platforms. My responsibilities included data extraction, transformation, mapping, cleansing, validation, UAT, migration execution, and post-migration quality checks. I worked with platforms such as Practice Evolve, Actionstep, and NetDocuments, using SQL, Excel, Power Query, and Python to support migration and data quality activities.

Before moving into data migration, I worked in audience measurement and data classification, where I developed experience in data quality control, large-scale data processing, BI reporting, and process improvement. I used Python, SQL, Power BI, Spotfire, and AWS-related tools to investigate data issues, monitor processes, and improve operational workflows.

I enjoy working with data from the point of extraction through transformation, validation, and reporting. I am particularly interested in building reliable data pipelines, automating repetitive processes, improving data quality, and turning complex datasets into useful insights.

I am also continuously expanding my technical skills in data engineering, cloud technologies, automation, and modern data platforms. I enjoy learning new technologies and applying them to practical problems.
